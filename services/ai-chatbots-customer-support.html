Frequently asked questions

Can the chatbot answer questions about my business?

Yes, it can be configured around approved service details, policies and FAQs. Answers should be tested and unclear questions should have a sensible fallback.

Can it book appointments or check live order status?

Only when a supported calendar, booking system, store or order platform is available and the required access is approved.

Can the bot collect leads?

Yes. A lead flow can ask relevant questions and route submissions through the agreed contact or CRM process.

What is the difference between an AI chatbot and an AI agent?

An AI chatbot is usually designed for interactive conversations, such as answering questions or collecting enquiries. An AI agent is designed to complete or assist with defined tasks using approved information and supported tools. Some solutions combine both capabilities.

Can you build an AI assistant using our business documents?

Where the selected tools support it, an assistant can use approved documents and knowledge sources to answer questions or help staff find information. Access permissions, data handling, testing and limitations are agreed before launch.

Can an AI agent take actions automatically?

Only within the agreed permissions and safeguards. Sensitive, customer-facing or external actions can require human approval, and integrations depend on supported tools and access.

Can I add a chatbot to my business website to answer customer questions?

Yes. We can configure an AI chatbot around your approved business information, common questions, knowledge sources and enquiry process. Language options, lead capture and connections to other systems depend on the agreed scope and supported integrations, with a clear hand-off for questions that need a person.
