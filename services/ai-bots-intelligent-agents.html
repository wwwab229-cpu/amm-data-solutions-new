Frequently asked questions

What can an AI bot or agent help with?

Depending on the agreed scope, an AI bot or agent can help retrieve approved information, summarize content, prepare drafts, classify requests or support repeatable tasks. Suitability depends on the model, tools, data and safeguards.

Can an AI agent work with our existing tools?

Where supported, an agent can connect to tools you already use. Access, data flow, permissions and integrations are agreed and tested as part of the project scope.

Can an AI agent act without human approval?

Only when the agreed setup, permissions and safeguards allow it. Sensitive, external or customer-facing actions can require human approval before execution.

Can you automate repetitive tasks in my business?

Yes, where the task, tools and permissions are suitable. AMM Data Solutions can assess repeatable work such as sorting requests, preparing summaries or moving approved information between supported tools. The workflow is tested, and sensitive or external actions can require human approval.
