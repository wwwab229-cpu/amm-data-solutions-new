Website chatbots: AI-powered and simple chat options

We can add a website chat experience that fits your business, visitor needs and budget. Choose an AI-powered assistant for more flexible conversations or a simple rule-based chat widget for guided questions and quick contact options.

AI-powered website chatbot

An AI assistant configured around your approved business information and agreed use cases. It can help visitors explore services, find relevant information and submit enquiries through a conversational experience.

  • Responds to visitor questions using an agreed knowledge source
  • Explains services, processes and frequently asked questions
  • Guides visitors towards relevant pages or enquiry forms
  • Can support multiple languages where the selected tools allow
  • Can connect to approved workflows or integrations when included in scope

Simple website chat widget

A lightweight, rule-based chat experience for businesses that need clear options rather than AI-generated answers. Visitors follow prepared prompts and menus to reach the information or contact route they need.

  • Welcome messages and predefined replies
  • Clickable options, menus and frequently asked questions
  • Guidance to service pages, email or contact forms
  • Basic enquiry collection where supported
  • A straightforward option for focused visitor journeys

Chatbot type, content setup, language options, integrations, third-party subscriptions and ongoing usage are scoped separately where applicable. We confirm the suitable option and any additional cost before work begins. For standalone chatbot and agent projects, see AI Chatbots & Intelligent Agents.

Frequently asked questions

Can you build a new website for my business?

Yes. We can plan and build a business website or landing page based on your content, goals, features and agreed scope.

Can you improve my existing website without redesigning everything?

Yes. We can review the current website and focus on specific improvements while preserving the existing look and structure where requested.

Can you add an AI chatbot or a simple chat widget to my website?

Yes. We can integrate an AI-powered chatbot configured around approved business information, or a simple rule-based chat widget with prepared messages, menus and contact options. The best fit depends on your needs, and setup, integrations and any third-party usage costs are agreed in advance.

Do you guarantee search rankings or a specific performance score?

No. We can apply sound development and SEO practices, test agreed improvements and report findings, but rankings and performance scores depend on many factors outside any provider's control.
