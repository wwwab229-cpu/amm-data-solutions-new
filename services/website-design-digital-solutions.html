Frequently asked questions

Will my website work on mobile?

Responsive behaviour is part of the agreed build and should be tested on common mobile and desktop viewports before release.

Can you connect forms or other tools?

Supported integrations can be included when access, data flow and requirements are clear and part of the project scope.

Do you guarantee a particular PageSpeed score?

No fixed score is guaranteed. Performance depends on assets, integrations, hosting and third-party scripts, and should be measured after implementation.
