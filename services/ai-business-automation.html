Frequently asked questions

What tasks can be automated?

Common candidates include repetitive data handling, notifications, routing and structured information processing. Suitability depends on your tools, data and approval requirements.

Will automation replace my existing systems?

Not necessarily. Where practical, workflows can connect supported tools you already use. Any new system or integration is agreed in the project scope.

Can an AI workflow take actions automatically?

Only where the agreed setup, permissions and safeguards support it. Sensitive or customer-facing actions can retain a human approval step.
