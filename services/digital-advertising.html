Frequently asked questions

Which advertising platforms do you manage?

AMM manages Google Ads, Meta Ads for Facebook and Instagram, LinkedIn Ads, Microsoft Advertising, TikTok Ads and ChatGPT Ads where official advertiser access is available. Each campaign is subject to platform requirements, advertiser permissions and target-market availability.

What do I need to provide?

Share your business details, website or product information if available, target audience, advertising budget and campaign goal. You can also tell us which platforms you prefer. AMM handles the agreed strategy, setup, creative preparation, launch, monitoring, optimization and reporting.

Will AMM manage the campaigns for me?

Yes. AMM provides end-to-end campaign management through authorized AMM-managed advertiser accounts or platform-supported linked accounts. If a platform requires client-specific verification, ownership or permissions, AMM will guide that step. You do not need to manage the campaigns yourself.

Is advertising spend included in the management fee?

No. The management fee pays for AMM’s service. The advertising budget is separate, confirmed with you in advance and funded separately. We do not guarantee a specific number of leads, sales or return because results depend on the offer, audience, budget, competition, tracking and landing-page experience.

Can you manage ChatGPT Ads for my business?

AMM includes ChatGPT Ads in its managed advertising service where official advertiser access is available to AMM and the target market. Campaign activation remains subject to OpenAI’s advertiser access, approval and platform requirements.
