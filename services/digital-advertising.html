Digital Advertising

AMM Data Solutions helps plan and manage digital advertising around a clear business objective, relevant audience and measurement approach. Platform availability, account access, creative requirements and budget are agreed before launch.

What this service can include

Scope and deliverables

Campaign planning, setup and optimization for Google Ads, Meta Ads and TikTok Ads, with tracking and reporting based on available access.

  • Campaign planning and audience strategy
  • Google Ads setup and management
  • Meta Ads for Facebook and Instagram
  • TikTok Ads where the account and market are eligible
  • Conversion tracking and performance review where supported
  • Reporting and ongoing optimization

Built around your business

We agree the scope, tools, access and priorities with you first. The final setup depends on your requirements, supported systems and the permissions available for the project.

AMM DS does not promise a specific result before understanding the requirements. Any third-party subscription, advertising spend or external integration is discussed as part of the scope.

Frequently asked questions

Which advertising platforms do you support?

Scope can include Google Ads, Meta Ads and TikTok Ads, depending on account access, market eligibility and campaign requirements.

Can you promise a specific number of leads or sales?

No. Results depend on factors including offer, audience, budget, competition, tracking and landing-page experience.

Do you run ChatGPT or AI-platform ads?

Only if an official advertising option is available and the business/account is eligible at the time. It is not assumed to be available everywhere.
