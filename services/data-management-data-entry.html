Frequently asked questions

Can you work with existing spreadsheets?

Yes, where the files can be safely shared and the required output and validation rules are agreed.

How do you handle data quality?

Checks can include formatting, required fields, duplicates and consistency rules that fit the task. Exceptions should be reported rather than silently guessed.

Can recurring data work be automated?

Where the process and tools are suitable, repetitive steps can be streamlined or automated as a separate agreed scope.
