Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This policy explains how AMM Data Solutions handles information submitted through the website's contact form and feedback system.

Information you provide

  • Contact requests: your name, email address, and message, if you choose to submit them through the contact form.
  • Feedback: the feedback message and any optional name, business or role, and star rating you choose to provide.

How information is used

Contact details are used to review and respond to enquiries. Feedback is used to understand experiences and improve our services. Feedback submissions are held for review; only feedback approved by AMM Data Solutions may be displayed publicly. Names and ratings are optional in the feedback form.

Service providers

The contact form is processed by Formspree, and the feedback system uses Supabase. Information submitted through these features is transmitted to those providers to deliver the requested functionality and may be processed under their own privacy and security terms. Their services may also generate technical logs needed to operate and protect the service.

Sharing and publication

We do not intentionally make pending feedback public. If feedback is approved, the message and any name, business or role, or rating included with it may appear on this website. Do not include information you do not want published in feedback intended for public display.

Security and retention

We use access controls and technical safeguards intended to protect submitted information. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Information is retained as needed to handle enquiries, moderate feedback, operate the service, and meet applicable obligations.

Your choices and requests

You may choose not to provide optional feedback details. If you want to ask about, correct, or request deletion of information you submitted, contact AMM Data Solutions using the email contact option on the main website. We may need enough information to identify the submission and will handle requests where we are able to do so.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when website features or data practices change. The date above indicates the latest revision.
