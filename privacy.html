Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This policy describes how AMM Data Solutions handles information submitted through its website, feedback tools and WhatsApp business communication workflows, and limited website-usage information collected through analytics.

Information you provide

  • Website enquiries: name, email address and message you submit through our contact form.
  • Feedback: optional name, business or role, rating, written feedback and related details you choose to provide.
  • WhatsApp messages: messages and related conversation information you send to our WhatsApp business number, such as message content, timestamps, message identifiers and available sender/contact details.
  • Lead and service details: business information, requested services, inquiry summaries, preferences or next-step details that you share and that may be recorded to help handle your inquiry.

WhatsApp communication and AI-assisted drafts

When you contact AMM Data Solutions through WhatsApp, messages may be processed and stored in our internal workspace to manage inquiries, maintain conversation history, prepare responses, identify requests that need human attention and support customer service.

AI-assisted tools may be used to generate suggested replies or summarize conversation context. Suggested replies are reviewed in our workflow; a draft is not itself a sent message. Our workspace may use human review and approval controls before any outbound message is sent. Automated messaging features, if enabled in a future configuration, will operate according to the controls and applicable messaging requirements in place at that time.

WhatsApp message data may be processed by Meta/WhatsApp and by infrastructure or service providers used to operate our communication workspace, including database and hosting providers. Those providers process data under their own terms and policies.

How information is used

We use information to respond to inquiries, understand business needs, provide requested services, maintain relevant records, prepare and review responses, improve our workflows, protect the service and handle support requests.

Website analytics

We use Microsoft Clarity and Google Analytics 4 to understand website usage and improve usability. These services may collect device, browser, page interaction, referral and approximate location information, and may use cookies or similar technologies. Avoid entering personal or sensitive details into analytics fields or URLs. You can manage cookies through your browser settings.

Service providers

Website contact forms are processed by Formspree, feedback and parts of our internal workspace use Supabase, hosting may use Vercel, and website analytics use Microsoft Clarity and Google Analytics. WhatsApp communication uses Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform. Information may be processed by these providers as needed to deliver the relevant functionality and protect their services.

Sharing and publication

We do not intentionally publish private WhatsApp conversations or pending feedback on our public website. Feedback publication is subject to review and applicable permissions. We do not sell personal information to advertisers.

Security and retention

We use access controls and technical safeguards intended to protect information. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. We retain information for as long as reasonably needed to handle inquiries, maintain relevant business records, operate our services, resolve issues and meet applicable obligations, then delete or otherwise handle it as appropriate.

Your choices and data requests

You may choose not to provide optional information. You may request access, correction or deletion of information you have shared with AMM Data Solutions by emailing ammdatasolutions229@gmail.com. Please identify the information or conversation you are referring to without sending passwords or other sensitive details. We will review and respond to requests where we are able to do so and where applicable requirements permit.

For WhatsApp communications, you can also stop messaging us. Please be aware that information already processed by Meta/WhatsApp may be subject to their own privacy practices and controls.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services and data practices change. The date above indicates the latest revision.
