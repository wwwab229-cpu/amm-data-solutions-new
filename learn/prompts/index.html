Business & communication

Use these prompts to make everyday writing clearer.

Writing · Beginner

Professional email assistant

Use when you need a clear, polite email and want the AI to ask for missing details rather than invent them.

Act as a professional business communication assistant. Help me write a clear, polite and professional email. First, ask me for: - The purpose of the email - Who will receive it - The key points that must be included - The tone I want Do not invent names, dates, promises or facts. If important information is missing, ask before drafting. After writing the email, provide a concise subject line and check the message for clarity and tone.

How to use it

  1. Paste the prompt into your preferred AI assistant.
  2. Answer its questions with the details of your email.
  3. Review all names, dates, facts and commitments before sending.
Productivity · Beginner

Organise a daily task list

Useful when your tasks are scattered and you need a realistic plan. It asks the AI not to assume deadlines or available time.

Help me organise my tasks into a realistic daily plan. Here is my task list: [Paste your tasks] My available working hours: [Add start and end time] Known deadlines: [Add deadlines, or write "not specified"] Please: 1. Group related tasks. 2. Identify urgent tasks only when a deadline or clear reason supports it. 3. Estimate effort as a rough suggestion, not a fact. 4. Ask me about missing deadlines or unclear priorities. 5. Include reasonable breaks and flexible time. 6. Present the plan in a simple table. 7. Do not schedule more work than the available time allows.

How to use it

  1. Replace the bracketed fields with your real information.
  2. Check the suggested effort and priority.
  3. Adjust the plan to match your actual commitments.
Research · Beginner

Understand a topic before making a decision

Use this to structure your learning. It does not replace checking original sources.

Help me understand this topic: [Insert topic]. Explain it for a beginner using: - A short plain-language overview - Key terms and what they mean - A practical everyday example - Potential benefits and limitations - Questions I should investigate further Separate established facts from assumptions. Do not invent statistics, sources or quotations. If you provide current or time-sensitive information, identify reliable original sources I should verify.
Privacy reminder: Do not paste passwords, payment details, confidential client records or sensitive personal information into an AI tool unless you have checked the tool’s privacy terms and have permission to share the data.
