Beginner roadmap · Daily productivity

Turn a scattered task list into a clearer day.

You do not need a complicated AI system to get started. Begin with a task list, ask an AI assistant to organise it, then review the plan before acting.

What you will learn

  • How to give an AI assistant useful task context.
  • How to ask for priorities without letting it invent deadlines.
  • How to turn a rough list into a schedule you can review.

Tools: ChatGPT or another AI assistant with text input. A calendar or task manager is optional.

Time: About 10–15 minutes for a first trial.

Your step-by-step roadmap

1

Collect your tasks

Write down the tasks you actually need to do. Include deadlines only when you know them. Keep private or confidential information out of the prompt.

2

Tell the AI your available time

Share your working hours, fixed appointments and any breaks you need. If you are unsure about effort, ask the AI to label estimates as suggestions.

3

Ask it to organise the list

Use the prompt below. Ask it to group related tasks, highlight known deadlines and identify missing information.

4

Review and correct the plan

Check whether the order makes sense, the time estimates are realistic and no important commitment is missing. AI can misunderstand context.

5

Move the approved plan into your calendar

Add only the tasks you agree with. Keep some flexible time and adjust the plan as your day changes.

Copy-ready prompt

Replace the bracketed fields with your information before using it.

Help me organise my tasks into a realistic daily plan. My tasks: [Paste your task list] Available time: [Start and end time] Fixed appointments: [List appointments] Known deadlines: [List deadlines, or say "not specified"] Please group related tasks, highlight only confirmed deadlines, estimate effort as a suggestion, ask about missing information, include breaks and flexible time, and present a schedule that fits my available hours. Do not invent deadlines or assume every task must be completed today.

What impact can this have?

  • A clearer view of what needs attention.
  • Less manual effort spent formatting and sorting a task list.
  • A useful starting point for planning the day.
This workflow does not guarantee that you will finish more tasks or save a fixed amount of time. Its value depends on your task list, the quality of context you provide and how carefully you review the result.

Limitations and good practice

  • AI may misunderstand urgency or context.
  • Estimated task durations can be wrong.
  • Never treat an AI-generated plan as a replacement for your own judgement.
  • Do not share sensitive personal, financial or confidential work data unnecessarily.
