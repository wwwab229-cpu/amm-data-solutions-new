Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more about AMM DS, AI automation, WhatsApp workflows and digital services for your business.

Do I need technical knowledge to use AI automation?+
No. AMM DS explains each solution in plain language and helps you understand how to use your AI automation or digital tool.
How long does an AI automation or website project take?+
AMM DS aims to deliver solutions efficiently. The timeline depends on your project’s size, requirements and complexity.
Can WhatsApp AI automation send customer messages without approval?+
The WhatsApp assistant workflow described here drafts replies and waits for your approval before sending. Automation rules depend on the final setup you choose.
Which languages does AMM DS support?+
AMM DS provides language support according to its service policies and the capabilities of the tools used for each project. Contact us to confirm language availability for your requirements.
Which advertising platforms does AMM DS support?+
AMM DS can plan and manage eligible Google Ads, Meta Ads for Facebook and Instagram, LinkedIn Ads, Microsoft Advertising and TikTok Ads campaigns. ChatGPT Ads support depends on official advertiser access and availability in your target market. Account permissions, platform eligibility and advertising spend are confirmed before launch.
How much do AI automation and digital solutions cost?+
Pricing depends on the project scope, features, integrations and ongoing support needed. Share your requirements with AMM DS to request a clear estimate.
