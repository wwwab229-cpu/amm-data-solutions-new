## Visibility principles
1. Build around real user needs and the seven actual services; do not create thin keyword/location doorway pages.
2. Keep business facts consistent: official name, service names, service scope, contact route, markets served and platform eligibility.
3. Make important content crawlable, indexable, internally linked, useful in plain language and consistent with visible page content.
4. Treat structured data as a machine-readable description of visible facts, not a ranking guarantee.
5. Treat `llms.txt` as an optional supplementary discovery document, not a Google ranking factor or a substitute for crawlable pages.
6. Earn external references through useful resources, genuine work samples, accurate directory profiles, relevant partnerships and authentic mentions. Do not buy or manufacture reviews, links or citations.
7. Measure search impressions, clicks, query-to-page relevance, qualified enquiries, referral sources and verified AI citations separately. Do not infer leads or revenue from impressions.
8. Preserve the current homepage layout, theme, logo usage and interaction patterns during SEO and visibility work.

## Work phases
### Phase 1 — Website and entity consistency
- Keep all seven service pages, homepage cards, schema, sitemap, `llms.txt`, social metadata and profile descriptions aligned.
- Validate every sitemap URL, canonical target, title, description, H1, internal links and structured data.
- Resolve duplicate, stale or contradictory business information before expanding content.

### Phase 2 — Search engine discovery
- Verify sitemap processing and URL indexing in Google Search Console.
- Review crawl/indexing errors, manual actions, query/page performance and the generative-AI visibility report if available for the property.
- Verify Bing Webmaster Tools ownership, sitemap ingestion and IndexNow support; submit changed canonical URLs through supported owner tools.
- Check other relevant crawlers and CDN/hosting access without opening private admin routes.

### Phase 3 — Authority and useful content
- Build one practical content cluster for each of the seven services, with original examples, process explanations, limitations, FAQs and clear paths to the relevant service page.
- Use the AMM workflow and actual demonstrations as evidence only where the demo is clearly labelled and no client outcome is implied.
- Publish at a sustainable quality-controlled pace; avoid mass-produced, repetitive or unsupported articles.

### Phase 4 — Cross-platform brand presence
- Audit the existing social profiles and agency/business listings for exact brand name, logo, service taxonomy, website URL, service descriptions and contact method.
- Prioritize platforms where target customers actively discover providers; do not create duplicate or low-quality listings merely to increase listing count.
- Reuse core facts consistently while adapting the format and examples to each platform.

### Phase 5 — Measurement and iteration
- Establish a dated baseline for organic search, Bing discovery, referral traffic, branded searches, social discovery and qualified enquiries.
- Review monthly and prioritize changes by verified reach, relevance and conversion impact.
- Report verified citations/mentions separately from model-generated recommendations or unverified AI visibility tests.
