# AMM Data Solutions — Brand Identity Guidelines

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Working master reference  
**Brand tagline:** Let's Make Solutions  
**Primary brand name:** AMM Data Solutions  
**Approved short form:** AMM DS  
**Website:** https://amm-data-solutions-new.vercel.app/

> This document is the master reference for consistent AMM Data Solutions branding across its website, social channels, client-facing documents, portfolio, directory profiles and future marketing. It does not authorize changes to the approved website layout, visual design or service prices.

## 1. Brand foundation

### Brand essence
Practical AI-powered and digital solutions that help organizations simplify work, improve operations and make better use of their data and digital tools.

### Positioning statement
AMM Data Solutions is a Pakistan-based digital solutions brand serving small businesses, startups, agencies, e-commerce businesses and growing organizations in Pakistan and international markets. It provides practical AI automation, search visibility, digital advertising, intelligent assistants, website development, data management and creative design services.

### Tagline
**Let's Make Solutions**

Use the tagline as a supporting brand line, not as a substitute for the company name. Do not repeat it in every card, footer item or page heading.

### Core promise
Practical digital solutions that help businesses work smarter.

This is a positioning promise, not a guarantee of specific financial, ranking, traffic or performance outcomes.

### Brand principles
1. **Practicality:** explain what the solution does and the business problem it addresses.
2. **Clarity:** use direct, understandable language and transparent scope.
3. **Trust:** make only verifiable claims; distinguish estimates, examples and completed work.
4. **Responsibility:** respect platform rules, client ownership, permissions and data privacy.
5. **Adaptability:** serve different business sizes and markets without overstating capabilities.
6. **Consistency:** use one recognizable identity across every public touchpoint.

## 2. Audience

Primary audiences:
- Small and growing businesses
- Startups and founders
- Agencies and service providers
- E-commerce businesses and online sellers
- Teams seeking to improve repetitive workflows, websites, data processes or digital visibility

Geographic positioning:
- Pakistan
- International markets where the project scope, platform access, language and client requirements can be supported

Do not imply that AMM has offices, staff, legal registrations, certifications or local branches in locations unless independently confirmed.

## 3. Official service architecture

AMM Data Solutions has exactly seven official service categories. Keep this order consistent on the website, company profile, social descriptions, proposals and service catalogues.

1. **AI Automation** — Practical workflow automation and AI-powered systems for business and everyday work.
2. **Google SEO & AI Search Visibility** — Technical and content SEO, discoverability and clear information architecture for search and AI-assisted discovery.
3. **Digital Advertising** — Advertising strategy and campaign management across supported platforms, subject to account access and market eligibility.
4. **AI Chatbots & Intelligent Agents** — Conversational assistants and task-focused agents designed around approved workflows and integrations.
5. **Website Development & Improvement** — Business websites, improvements, responsive implementation and relevant integrations.
6. **Data Management & Data Entry** — Organizing, processing, maintaining and entering business information with agreed quality checks.
7. **Creative Design & Digital Content** — Design assets and digital content aligned with the client's brief and brand.

Do not create extra top-level service categories or duplicate cards. Individual deliverables may appear as sub-services under the relevant official category.

### Advertising transparency
- If a client already has an advertising account, AMM requests the required authorized access.
- If the client has no account, AMM helps establish an appropriate client-specific account where platform rules permit.
- Account ownership, permissions and billing must remain transparent and consistent with platform policies.
- Advertising spend and AMM management fees must be shown separately.
- ChatGPT Ads and other emerging platform offerings must be described according to actual availability, access and eligibility at the time of publication.
- Never promise ad results, guaranteed leads, sales or return on ad spend.

## 4. Visual identity

### Approved visual direction
Use the established clean, professional identity:
- White and light neutral backgrounds
- Black or near-black primary text
- Yellow as the signature accent
- Neutral grey for supporting text, borders and secondary surfaces
- Clear spacing, strong readability and restrained use of decorative elements

The website's existing approved design and layout must be preserved unless the owner explicitly authorizes a redesign.

### Working colour references
These are references observed in the current homepage source and the approved visual direction. They are not a replacement for sampling the official master logo file.

| Role | Working value | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Primary text | `#111111` | Main headings and high-contrast text |
| Signature yellow | `#FFD83D` | Highlight, key accent and emphasis |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Main surfaces and negative space |
| Light neutral | `#FAFAFF` | Soft page background where already used |
| Supporting grey | Use a neutral grey with accessible contrast | Secondary text, dividers and quiet UI |

**Important reconciliation note:** legacy purple/lime tokens remain in parts of the current stylesheet. They are not to be expanded into new brand materials. Do not change the live website stylesheet as part of this guideline document. A separate, deliberate cleanup should only happen after checking the approved live design and receiving authorization.

### Typography
The current homepage references:
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** for prominent headings
- **Inter** for body copy and interface text

Use a clear hierarchy, readable line lengths and consistent weights. Avoid decorative typefaces for business-critical information.

### Logo rules
- Use only the official AMM Data Solutions wordmark/logo asset supplied by the owner.
- Preserve the original proportions, spacing, colours and transparent background.
- Do not redraw, recolour, stretch, rotate, add effects to or invent an emblem for the logo.
- Keep sufficient clear space around the logo.
- Use a high-contrast approved version against the selected background.
- Do not use a cropped or blurry logo when the original transparent PNG is available.
- Do not treat “AMM” alone as the full public brand name.

## 5. Verbal identity and writing style

### Voice
Modern, trustworthy, friendly, professional and direct. Explain the value in plain language. Be confident without sounding exaggerated or overly technical.

### Preferred language
- “We build practical AI-powered tools and digital solutions.”
- “Simplify repetitive work.”
- “Improve how your business gets things done.”
- “Solutions shaped around your requirements.”
- “Clear scope, transparent process and practical implementation.”

### Avoid
- “Guaranteed #1 rankings”
- “Guaranteed sales or leads”
- “World's best” or “number one” without independently verifiable evidence
- Invented client counts, revenue figures, case studies, testimonials or awards
- Claims that every AI model already knows or recommends AMM
- Overuse of buzzwords such as revolutionary, limitless, cutting-edge or game-changing
- Calling estimates fixed prices or presenting estimates as guaranteed quotations

### Company description — short
AMM Data Solutions provides practical AI automation and digital services, including SEO, advertising, intelligent assistants, website development, data management and creative design.

### Company description — medium
AMM Data Solutions is a Pakistan-based digital solutions brand helping small businesses, startups, agencies, e-commerce businesses and growing organizations improve how work gets done. Its seven service areas cover AI automation, Google SEO and AI search visibility, digital advertising, AI chatbots and intelligent agents, website development and improvement, data management and data entry, and creative design and digital content.

### Company description — extended
AMM Data Solutions helps businesses turn practical needs into workable digital solutions. The company focuses on AI-powered automation, search visibility, digital advertising, intelligent assistants, website development, data processes and creative design. It works with small businesses, startups, agencies, e-commerce businesses and growing organizations in Pakistan and international markets. Projects are scoped around the client's requirements, available tools, permissions and delivery needs. The brand promise is simple: practical digital solutions that help businesses work smarter.

## 6. Brand consistency across platforms

Every public profile should use, where supported:
- Display name: **AMM Data Solutions**
- Official logo asset
- Tagline: **Let's Make Solutions**
- Website: https://amm-data-solutions-new.vercel.app/
- A matching short or medium company description
- The same seven service categories and order where services are listed
- Consistent brand colours and tone

Do not create duplicate profiles to occupy more search results. Do not add an address, phone number, registration number, certification or opening hours unless confirmed and approved by the owner. Do not publish private contact information.

### Social content themes
1. Practical AI automation and workflow education
2. Search visibility and website improvement explainers
3. Advertising education and transparent campaign operations
4. Intelligent assistant use cases
5. Website and data process tips
6. Creative design examples
7. Real project updates and approved case studies

Use original examples and clearly label demonstrations or illustrative mockups. Obtain client permission before publishing names, logos, screenshots, testimonials or project details.

## 7. Trust, portfolio and proof standards

Only publish evidence that exists and is approved:
- Completed project screenshots
- Live project links
- Client names and logos with permission
- Testimonials in the client's own words with permission
- Verifiable qualifications, registrations and awards
- Case studies with the actual scope, context and results

For each case study, record:
1. Client and permission status
2. Business context and problem
3. Agreed scope and service category
4. What AMM delivered
5. Delivery date or period
6. Evidence that can be shown publicly
7. Results only where measured and attributable
8. Limitations or external factors where relevant

Never fabricate portfolio work, reviews, client logos, statistics or outcomes.

## 8. Search and AI discovery standards

The brand should be represented consistently and accurately wherever AMM has a legitimate public presence.

Priorities:
- Crawlable, useful website pages
- Clear company and service descriptions
- Consistent Organization, WebSite and service information
- Valid canonical URLs, sitemap and robots directives
- Descriptive page titles, headings, internal links and image alt text
- Helpful original articles and service guides
- Genuine third-party references and client mentions
- Accurate and maintained profiles on relevant platforms

Structured data must reflect visible, factual page content. Do not add fake reviews, fake ratings, unsupported credentials or misleading business details.

An `llms.txt` file may help communicate site structure to systems that choose to read it, but it is not a guaranteed ranking factor or a way to force AI systems to include the brand. No external model can be guaranteed to ingest, remember or recommend AMM.

## 9. Client data, privacy and account integrity

- Collect only the information needed for the agreed service.
- Do not publish client information without permission.
- Use authorized account access and least-privilege permissions.
- Keep client advertising accounts and billing transparent.
- Never ask clients to share passwords in public messages or documents.
- Explain what data an automation or AI tool processes and where relevant limitations apply.
- Obtain approval before sending messages, publishing content or taking external actions on a client's behalf.

## 10. Approval and change control

This guideline is the master reference for future brand work. Before changing a public asset, check it against:
1. Official company name and logo
2. Approved colour and typography direction
3. Seven-service taxonomy and order
4. Truthful claims and available proof
5. Contact and privacy rules
6. Platform policies
7. Owner-approved website design and prices

Changes to the website's visual design, layout or prices require separate explicit approval. Business Profile work remains deferred until the owner requests it.
