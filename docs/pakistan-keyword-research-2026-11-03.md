# Pakistan Keyword Research & Page Targeting — AMM Data Solutions

**Research date:** 2026-11-03  
**Property:** https://amm-data-solutions-new.vercel.app/  
**Primary market:** Pakistan  
**Secondary market:** Relevant international English-language searches  
**Scope:** Existing six service pages and their six supporting resource guides. This is a research and prioritization document; it does not authorize changing live website copy or design.

## Executive summary

This research creates an evidence-led first keyword set for AMM Data Solutions without requiring a pre-existing keyword report. It combines:
- Google autocomplete-style suggestions retrieved with Keyword Tool guest access, explicitly set to Pakistan and English.
- Live web search result sampling and competitor page review for the six service categories.
- The connected Google Search Console property and page-level performance for 2026-10-04 through 2026-10-31.
- Existing service-page intent ownership and the supporting resource-guide structure.

**Important metric limitation:** Keyword Tool returned suggestion phrases, but no cached search-volume/competition metrics for the sampled terms. The connected VibeSEO SEO tools reported that the account has no active plan including SEO tools. Ahrefs Keywords Explorer previously returned an “Insufficient plan” response. Therefore, this document does not invent monthly search volume, keyword difficulty, or claims that a phrase is low competition. Keyword suggestions and competitor visibility are evidence of phrasing and market positioning, not proof of search volume or rank difficulty.

## Research method and confidence

| Evidence | What it supports | What it does not prove |
|---|---|---|
| Keyword Tool Google autocomplete, Pakistan, English | Suggested query wording and related variants | Search volume or ranking difficulty where metrics are absent |
| Live web SERP sampling | Which competitor pages and page angles appear for sampled queries | A complete or fixed ranking list; rankings vary by location, device and time |
| Google Search Console | Actual impressions, clicks, CTR and average position for AMM pages | Total market demand; query details may be withheld at low volumes |
| Existing page titles, URLs and guides | Current site intent and URL ownership | That a page is already ranking for every mapped phrase |

Search Console data is finalized with an approximately three-day delay. The page review below uses the connected property's effective range **2026-10-04 to 2026-10-31**.

## Cross-service keyword ownership rules

1. **AI agents vs chatbot:** AI Bots owns internal workflows, repetitive tasks and business process automation. Website AI Chatbots owns website visitor questions, customer support and enquiry capture.
2. **Ads vs creative:** Digital Advertising owns campaign setup, management and optimization. Creative Design owns visual assets and social media post design.
3. **SEO vs ads:** SEO owns organic Google and AI-search visibility. Digital Advertising owns paid campaigns and ad spend.
4. **Data entry vs automation:** Data Management owns spreadsheet cleanup, formatting and data-entry tasks. AI Bots owns automated multi-step workflows that connect business tools.
5. Each resource guide owns informational intent and links to its corresponding commercial service page.
6. Do not create separate near-duplicate pages for every wording variant, city or language. Expand only when there is a distinct audience need and enough unique, useful information.

## SERP competition and opportunity assessment

This is a qualitative assessment based on sampled result pages, not a numeric keyword-difficulty score.

| Service | Qualitative assessment | Practical differentiation |
|---|---|---|
| AI automation | Competitive, with several Pakistan agencies positioning around agents and workflows | Clear small-business use cases, transparent scope, human review and practical handover |
| Website chatbots | Competitive, with dedicated Pakistan chatbot pages | Website-first scope, business-specific knowledge, language flexibility and safe escalation |
| SEO + AI visibility | Established, competitive agency category | Evidence-led SEO, honest reporting, no ranking guarantees, clear AI-search scope |
| Ads management | Established agency category | Clearly separated Google, Meta and TikTok campaign scope; explain ad spend separately |
| Social media design | Multiple specialist design studios and general agencies | Focused static design deliverables, brand consistency and business-ready templates |
| Excel/data services | Fragmented mix of specialists, freelancers and service companies | Business-focused spreadsheet cleanup, structure and repeatable data-management support |

**Priority order for page refinement (business-fit and SERP clarity, not search-volume ranking):**
1. Data Management — specific task phrases make the service scope easy to explain; exclude job intent.
2. Creative Design — concrete deliverables and a clear distinction from advertising.
3. Website AI Chatbots — clear buyer problem and distinct page intent.
4. Digital Advertising — clear campaign-management intent; separate from creative.
5. AI Chatbots & Intelligent Agents — valuable but broad category; use specific workflow examples.
6. SEO + AI Search Visibility — important core service, but broad SEO agency terms are established and require stronger evidence and differentiation.

## What to monitor next

1. Recheck GSC after the latest service-page FAQ and metadata changes have had time to be crawled and data has finalized.
2. Review query-to-page mapping separately for Pakistan and all countries. Do not merge country-filtered and all-country figures.
3. Watch impressions and actual queries for the mapped commercial phrases; check whether guides are attracting informational queries and linking visitors to their service page.
4. Watch for cannibalization: the same query repeatedly appearing for two different service pages.
5. Prioritize pages using actual impressions, clicks, CTR, average position, query relevance and enquiry quality—not just the number of suggested phrases.
6. If exact monthly volume and KD are needed later, obtain a Pakistan-targeted Keyword Planner export or an SEO data source with Pakistan metrics. Do not use global or worldwide metrics as a substitute for Pakistan.
