## 13. Publishing checklist

Before updating a public profile:
- Confirm the account is the official AMM account and the owner controls it.
- Use the official AMM Data Solutions logo, not a generated substitute.
- Use the exact company name and website URL.
- Choose one bio appropriate to the platform.
- Do not add unverified addresses, phone numbers, registration details or certifications.
- Do not claim guaranteed search rankings, leads, sales or AI recommendations.
- Do not claim ChatGPT Ads or another platform service is available unless actual access and eligibility have been confirmed.
- Keep the seven service categories consistent.
- Check the profile on mobile after saving.
- Record the final public profile URL in the brand asset register.
